Transcribe audio, right in your browser

Drop in a recording and get a transcript in English, German or Italian. Long recordings are sliced at natural pauses and transcribed piece by piece, with the text streaming in as each slice finishes, so even a two-hour file stays light on memory. The Whisper speech model runs on your device, so your audio never leaves this page and there is nothing to sign up for.

Transcript

With timestamps

  • How long files work: the audio is cut at natural pauses into slices of about 30 seconds. Each slice is transcribed on its own and then released from memory, so a two-hour recording needs about as much memory as a one-minute clip.
  • If the tab crashes or you stop a run halfway, everything transcribed so far stays on screen, ready to copy or download. Only the slice in progress is lost.
  • WebGPU: in Chrome and Edge the model can run on your graphics card, several times faster than on the processor. Other browsers fall back to the CPU automatically, and if the GPU path ever fails mid-run, the job continues on CPU from the same slice.
  • Large v3 Turbo is the most accurate model here and the best pick for German and Italian, but it is a big one-time download and slow on CPU alone. On a phone, start with Tiny or Base.
  • Nothing is uploaded anywhere. Closing the tab clears the recording from memory; the downloaded model stays cached for next time.
